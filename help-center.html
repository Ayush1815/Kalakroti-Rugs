Detailed Policies

At Kalakroti Rugs

Every rug is thoughtfully curated to reflect timeless craftsmanship, refined materials, and enduring design. We understand that selecting a handcrafted rug for your home is a meaningful investment, and we are committed to providing a transparent, dependable, and premium customer experience at every step.

Non-Returnable Items

The following items are not eligible for return or exchange:

  • Custom-made or personalized rugs
  • Made-to-order pieces
  • Clearance or final sale products
  • Rugs damaged due to improper use, mishandling, or unauthorized cleaning
  • Products showing visible signs of wear after delivery

Return Approval Process

Once your request is received, our support and quality assurance team will review it within 1–2 business days. If approved, a pickup will be arranged where service is available, or detailed return shipping instructions will be shared. Please note that all returned items undergo a final quality inspection before refund approval.

Damaged, Defective, or Incorrect Orders

We recommend inspecting your order immediately upon delivery. If you receive a damaged rug, an incorrect item, or a product with a manufacturing defect, please contact our support team within 48 hours of receiving the order and include clear photographs or videos for verification.

Our team will work promptly toward a suitable resolution through replacement, exchange, repair assistance, or refund (where applicable).
